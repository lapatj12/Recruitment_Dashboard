Where these numbers come from
  • Current Total Headcount / Manpower / HR Digest / Average Age / Average Tenure — live snapshot as of the last file update, counted directly from the "รายชื่อพนักงาน Manpower" and "รายชื่อพนักงาน HRD" sheets (every current row = one employee). Not filtered by year.
  • Net Headcount Change — "Started" is every row in the "Recruitment" sheet with status "เริ่มงานแล้ว", all-time (not limited to this year). "Resigned" is every row in the "Turnover" sheet, all-time. Both respect the Company filter above: hires by the Recruitment sheet's own company column, resignations by the employee ID prefix (MAN/HRD), falling back to an estimate from division for older-format IDs.
